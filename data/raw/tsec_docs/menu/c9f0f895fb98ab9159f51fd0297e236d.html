Important Judgments

S. No. Petition No. and date of Judgment Court Contents in brief
1 CIVIL APPEAL NO. 4615 OF 2023 dated 09/04/2024 Supreme Court of India Karikho Kri Vs Nuney Tayang and another.
Right to privacy of the candidate would still survive as regards matters which are of no concern to the voter or are irrelevant to his candidature for public office. In that respect, non-disclosure of each and every asset owned by a candidate would not amount to a defect, much less, a defect of a substantial character.
2 EP No 31 of 2019 dated 25/07/2023 High Court TS Mr. Jalagam Venkat Rao Vs Mr. Vanama Venkateswara Rao .
Declaring the election of Vanama Venkateswara Rao as returned candidate for 117 Kothagudem Assembly Constituency, Bhadradri Kothagudem District, Telangana State as Void for filing false affidavit.
3 SLP(C)No(s) 4895 - 4896/2023 dated 10/07/2023 Supreme Court of India J. Venkat Reddy Vs Ambadi Anji Yadav And ORS.
Dismissal of SLPs filed against the order dated 7.02.2023 in WP(PIL) No. 30/2022 and WP No 40739/2022 Passed by the High Court for the State of Telenagana at Hyderabad regarding Elections to Anthireddyguda Gram Panchayath, Nandigam Manadal Rangareddy District.
4 WPA (P) 250, 286, 287 of 2023 dated 13/06/2023 Others High Court of Calcutta :
Suvendu Adhikari and Another Vs The West Bengal State Election Commission And ORS.
Regarding filing of nominations, deploying Central Forces, prohibition of usage of contractual employees in elections etc. in elections to the Panchayats.
5 DIARY NO. 15999 of 2021 Arising out of SLP(C) NO 7414 of 2021 dated 14/09/2022 Supreme Court of India S. Rukmini Madegowda Vs The State Election Commission Karnataka And ORS.
False/wrong declaration of assets by candidate about his/her and spouse/dependents amount to corrupt practice and liable for disqualification
6 WP (C) 278 of 2022 dated 10/05/2022 Supreme Court of India Suresh Mahajan Vs State of Madhya Pradesh and ANR.
State Election Commissions shall complete the election process well before the expiry of five years term of the outgoing election body.
7 Miscellaneous Application Diary No 31495/2021 in SLP (C) No 20734/2021 dated 17/12/2021 Supreme Court of India Manmohan Nagar Vs The State of Madhya Pradesh and Ors.
The upper ceiling of 50 Percentage vertical reservations in favour of SCs/STs/OBC should not be breached / crossed in local bodies.
8 WP 15280 of 2021 dated 22/09/2021 Others High Court of Madhya Pradesh: Nagrik Upbbokta Margadarshak through its president Dr.P.G. Najpande and ors Vs The state of Madhya Pradesh and Ors.
Only Election Commission is competent to decide when to conduct bye-elections: Madhya Pradesh High Court.
9 WP 980 of 2019 dated 04/03/2021 Supreme Court of India Vikas Kishan rao Gawali Vs State of Maharashtra and Others.
Maharastra Local Body Elections - Triple Test for OBC Reservation in Local Body Elections - Exceeding 50 Percentage of Reservation in some Districts - Quashing and setting aside the Notification to that extent.
10 Special Civil Application No. 3261 of 2021 dated 18/02/2021 Others High Court of Gujarat at Ahmedabad: Life Insurance Corporation of India Vs Gujarat State Election Commission.
State Election Commission's Power, Authority and Jurisdiction to requisition and or appoint staff of LIC for Election Duty .
11 WP 22176 of 2020 dated 07/12/2020 High Court TS K.Antony reddy and another Vs The State of Telangana and Others.
Erroneous utilization of Distinguish Mark instead of Arrow Cross Mark on Ballot Paper in Ordinary Elections to GHMC 2020.
12 WP No 7987 of 2020 dated 13/11/2020 Others High Court of Karnataka at Bengaluru: Sri K.C. Kondaiah and Ors Vs the State of Karnataka and Ors.
Conduct of Local Body Elections - Powers and obligations of State Election Commission in the backdrop of postponement of Gram Panchayat Elections by the KSEC due to COVID-19 Pandemic.
13 WP (PIL) Nos. 84 and 87 of 2019, dated 22/10/2019 High Court TS K. Anuj kumar Reddy Vs State of Telangana and Ors and Dr. S. Malla Reddy Vs Satate of Telangana and Ors.
Delimitation of Wards and Reservations of seats made in Urban Local Bodies cannot be challenged in view of the embargo contained in Article 243-ZG of the Constitution of India, which bar courts to interfere in electoral matters
14 WP No. 27036 of 2018 and Batch dated 11/10/2018 High Court TS State Government shall extend full cooperation to the State Election Commission to enable the later to conduct elections. The SEC is granted liberty to request His Excellency the Governor of the State under clause (3) of Article 243-K to make available to it such staff as may be necessary for the discharge of its constitutional obligations.
15 WP (CIVIL) No 330 OF 2016 dated 26/09/2018 Supreme Court of India Lok Prohari through its General Secretary S N Shukla Vs Election Commission of India and Ors.
An offence attracting disqualification under Section 8 of RP Act, 1951.
16 SLP(c ) No. 16941/2017 dated 04/07/2017 Supreme Court of India The State of Telangana Vs Pandyam Naveen Kumar Yadav and ANR.
Elections to Pahadishareef Gram Panchayat of Saroornagar Mandal in Ranga Reddy District - Notification issued on 23.06.2017 - Honble Supreme Court of India stayed all other proceedings pursuant to the election notification.
17 W.A No. 845 of 2017 dated 30/06/2017 High Court TS Pandyam Naveen kumar Yadav Vs The State of Telangana and another.
Elections to Pahadishareef Gram Panchayat of Saroornagar Mandal in Ranga Reddy District - Election Notification issued - Honble High Court (single judge) stayed the election - In the Writ Appeal No. 845 of 2017, the High Court set aside the stay order and allowed the SEC to continue election process as per Notification.
18 WP No 21072 of 2017 dated 28/06/2017 High Court TS The State of Telangana Vs Telangana State Election Commission.
Stay order Dated 28.07.2017 on Election Notification Dated 23.06.2017 for a period of two weeks.
19 SCA 16313 of 2015 dated 21/10/2015 Others High Court of Gujarat at Ahmedabad: Virendra sin Mafagi Vaghela and ANR Vs Sate of Gujarat and ANR.
Judgment of High Court of Gujarat at Ahmedabad - Setting aside the decision of State Election Commission, Gujarat for postponement of the Elections of local bodies in the State.
20 Order dated 13/07/2014 Election Commission Of India Dr. Madhavrao Kinhalkar and ors Vs Ashok Shankar rao Chavan.
Account of Election Expenditure of Shri Ashok Chavan, returned candidate from 85-Bhokar Assembly Constituency at the general election to the Maharashtra Legislative Assembly, 2009 - Scrutiny of account under section 10 A of the Representation of the People Act, 1951 - Show cause notice issued to the respondent for failing to lodge account of election expenses in the manner required by the Act and Rules.
21 Civil Appeal No. 5044 of 2014 @ SLP (c) No. 29882 of 2011, Civil Appeal No. 5045 of 2014 @ SLP (c) No. 14209 of 2012 and Civil Appeal No. 5078 of 2014 @ SLP (C) No. 21958 of 2013, dated 05.05.2014 dated 05/05/2014 Supreme Court of India Ashok Shankar rao Chavan Vs Dr. Madhav rao Kinhalkar and Ors.
Election Commission can disqualify candidates for filing false or incorrect Election Expenditure Accounts.
22 Special Leave Appeal to (Civil) Nos. 38295-38296 / 2012 dated 18.02.2013 dated 18/02/2013 Supreme Court of India AP Most Backward Classes Committee ANR Vs Government of AP Others
Special Leave Appeal filed on the judgement and order dated 04.09.2012 in WP No. 16473/2011 WP No. 16560/2011 of the High Court of Andhra Pradesh, Hyderabad.
23 WRIT PETITION No.31639 of 2011 AND BATCH dated 04/09/2012 High Court TS Nimmaka Jaya Raj Vs The Government of Andhra Pradesh and others.
The challenge is to certain provisions of the Andhra Pradesh Panchayat Raj Act, 1994, relating to appointment of special officers to Panchayats and reservation to the offices of Mandal Praja Parishads and Zilla Praja Parishads. However, in PIL Nos.149 and 150 of 2012, the petitioner has challenged the definition of Backward Classes under the Andhra Pradesh Municipalities Act, 1965 and the Andhra Pradesh Municipal Corporations Act, 1994.
24 WRIT PETITION (CIVIL) NO. 356 OF 1994 with W.P. (C) NOS. 245 OF 1995 AND 517 OF 2005 dated 11/05/2010 Supreme Court of India Dr K Krishna Murthy Others Vs Union of India
The constitutional validity of some aspects of the reservation policy prescribed for the composition of elected local self-government.
25 WA No.709 of 2006 and batch dated 18/12/2006 High Court TS Upheld the provisions of the APPR Act, 1994 and Rules made there under with regard to preparation of electoral rolls of panchayats duly adopting the entries in the latest Assembly Rolls. Also upheld the procedure contemplated in the provisions of the Act and Rules to get the names included or deleted or corrected first in the Assembly Rolls under sections 22 and 23 of the RP Act, 1950 to make consequential amendments in the Panchayat Rolls upto the date of election notification of the panchayat concerned by the District Panchayat Officer concerned.
26 Special Civil Application No. 9847 of 2005 dated 19/10/2006 Supreme Court of India Kishan Singh Tomar Vs. Municipal Corporation of The City of Ahmedabad
This appeal is directed against the judgment of the Division Bench of the High Court of Gujarat praying for a writ of mandamus or any other appropriate writ or direction to the respondents in the writ petition, namely, the Municipal Corporation of the City of Ahmedabad, the State of Gujarat and the Gujarat State Election Commission, to take all steps necessary for the purpose of holding elections for constituting the Municipal Corporation of the city of Ahmedabad before the expiry of the duration of the Municipal Corporation constituted pursuant to the elections held in October, 2000
27 WA No.1321 of 2005 dated 22/08/2005 High Court TS The provisions of Xth schedule of the Constitution (anti-defection law) cannot be applied to local bodies.
28 WP No.13388 of 2003 dated 13/04/2004 High Court TS The elected body of Ramagundem Municipal Council shall continue for 5 years from the date appointed for the first meeting and no longer.
29 WP No.979 and 8966 of 2002 dated 27/03/2003 High Court TS The Court held that the term of elected body of Bukkarayasamudram Mandal Parishad shall be 5 years from the date appointed for the first meeting and no longer. The period during which the body was not functioning due to administrative reasons shall not result in extending the term beyond 5 years.
30 WP No.17232 of 2000 and batch dated 13/12/2000 High Court TS Court held that reservation of seats for B.Cs in Panchayat Raj Bodies by drawal of lots is bad in law and issued directions for identification of BCs in the voters lists for the purpose of reserving the seats in favour of BC in descending order and on rotation basis.
31 W.P.No.2481 of 2000 dated 21/04/2000 High Court TS Judgment Writ Petition Nos.2481, 2539, 2988, 3021, 3893, 4244, 4509, 4604 and 2690 of 2000.
32 CA No 3839 of 1982 dated 05/03/1984 Supreme Court of India A.C.Jose Vs. Sivan Pillai Ors When there is no parliamentary legislation or rule made thereunder, the Commission is free to pass any orders in respect of the conduct of elections, Where the Act or the Rules are silent, the Commission has no doubt plenary powers under Article 324to give any direction in respect of the conduct of election.
33 CA No 1297 of 1977 dated 02/12/1977 Supreme Court of India Mohinder Singh Gill Anr. Vs. The Chief Election Commissioner, New Delhi Provisions of Article 243K are pari-materia to those of Article 324 SEC enjoys the plenary powers like ECI. SEC is empowered to cope with situations that may not be provided in the enacted laws rules.
